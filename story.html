WHY “SLEEPY”?

Because good things
don't need to shout.

Sleepy Brew House is a small home-based takeaway coffee and matcha spot in Serangoon North. The idea is deliberately simple: make a drink worth pausing for, serve it in small windows, and let the drink do the talking.

The menu moves between familiar espresso drinks, matcha, hojicha and occasional collaborations. It is a neighbourhood ritual rather than a destination spectacle.
